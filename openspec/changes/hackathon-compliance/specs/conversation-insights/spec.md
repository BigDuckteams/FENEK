# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: LLM-written highlights take precedence
The system SHALL show the summary and the tasks written by the LLM during processing when the transcript has them, and SHALL otherwise compute them with the rule-based analysis.

#### Scenario: Transcript with LLM highlights
- **WHEN** a transcript stores an LLM summary and task list
- **THEN** "Коротко" and "Задачи и договорённости" show them, and questions still come from the rules

#### Scenario: Transcript without LLM highlights
- **WHEN** a transcript stores no LLM highlights (modes were off or the LLM failed)
- **THEN** the rule-based summary, tasks and questions are shown

#### Scenario: Source note
- **WHEN** the highlights card is shown
- **THEN** its note says whether the items were generated by the language model or selected by rules

### Requirement: Exports use the same highlights
The system SHALL use the same summary and tasks in the Markdown export as on screen.

#### Scenario: Markdown export
- **WHEN** a transcript with LLM highlights is exported to Markdown
- **THEN** its "Коротко" and "Задачи и договорённости" sections contain the LLM items

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Extractive summary
The system SHALL build a rule-based summary ("Коротко") of 2 to 5 sentences, growing with the square root of the sentence count, only for conversations of at least six sentences, choosing questions-free sentences of 6 to 45 words that are dense in words recurring across the conversation, favouring sentences with decision words ("договорились", "решили", "итог"), skipping near-duplicates, and presenting them in conversation order; it is used whenever the transcript has no LLM summary.

#### Scenario: Short conversation
- **WHEN** a conversation has fewer than six sentences
- **THEN** the summary is empty

#### Scenario: Duplicates
- **WHEN** two candidate sentences share more than half of their meaningful words
- **THEN** only the higher-ranked one is included

#### Scenario: Order
- **WHEN** the summary is shown
- **THEN** its sentences follow the order of the conversation, not the ranking
